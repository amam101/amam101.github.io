01 — Approach

From the server rack to the edge.

  1. /01

    Distill

    On-policy distillation teaches a small model to answer like a much larger one. With EDGE-OPD, the context it would normally look up is built into its weights, so it stays sharp offline.

  2. /02

    Compress

    Block sparsity, token pruning, lottery-ticket sub-networks, LoRA, quantization, TensorRT: the cuts that make a Transformer fit the hardware and run in real time.

  3. /03

    Measure

    Benchmarks, gold sets and evaluation pipelines for military, agentic, cyber and general capability, wired straight into training so every round is scored.

  4. /04

    Deploy

    Onto ground robots and quadrupeds through ROS 2 (with the US Army Research Laboratory ↗), onto UAVs, laptops and phones, and onto secure local hardware with no internet at all.
